How Cadence Operates

01

Connect Catalog & Bank

Enter your shop name, add product details (price, inventory status), and input your payout bank. ADA runs locally on your catalog only.

02

Share Your Custom Chat Link

Embed the unique link on your Instagram bio, WhatsApp, or Facebook page. Buyers click the link and enter the direct chat interface instantly.

03

Watch Ada Close the Loop

Ada handles dialogue, inventory verification, and displays your account number to finalize bank transfers. Real-time notifications arrive on your device.
